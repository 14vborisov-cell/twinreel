Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Club Room Limited ("Twinreel", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at twinreel.ai (the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name and email address provided via Google or Apple OAuth when you create an account

User-uploaded content

  • Photos you upload for video generation
  • Generated videos
  • Any content you post or share publicly on the platform

Payment information

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not collect or store your credit card number, bank account details, or other financial information. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy

Automatically collected information

  • We do not use cookies
  • We may collect basic server logs (IP address, browser type, access times) as part of standard web hosting

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Service
  • To process video generations from your uploaded photos
  • To process payments via Stripe
  • To pay creators their revenue share via Stripe Connect
  • To communicate with you about your account or transactions
  • To enforce our Terms of Service

3. Data Retention

  • Photos and videos you upload are stored on our servers indefinitely
  • Account information is retained for as long as your account exists
  • You can delete your account and associated data at any time through your account settings

4. Data Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Stripe — payment and payout processing
  • AI video model providers (Seedance, Kling, Hailuo, Veo, Runway, PixVerse) — your uploaded photo and the creator's prompt are sent to the selected provider to generate your video
  • Law enforcement — if required by law or valid legal process

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Third-Party AI Providers

When you purchase a video generation, your uploaded photo is sent to a third-party AI video model provider selected by the template creator. Each provider has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. Twinreel does not control how these providers process your data beyond the generation request.

6. Publicly Shared Content

If you post or share content publicly on Twinreel, that content is visible to other users of the platform. You are responsible for the content you choose to share publicly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at 14vborisov@gmail.com.

EU/UK users (GDPR): Our legal basis for processing your personal data is (a) performance of a contract when providing the Service, and (b) legitimate interest for security and service improvement.

California users (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of any sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Twinreel does not have a minimum age restriction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us at 14vborisov@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including countries where our servers, Stripe, and AI model providers are located. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the effective date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

The Product
A marketplace for social video content
Twinreel connects two sides of the short-form video economy.

Buyers

TikTok, Reels, and Shorts creators who want unique content for their profiles.

  • Browse templates for trending formats — dances, challenges, photoshoots, transformations
  • Upload a selfie and pay a one-time fee
  • Receive a video you fully own
  • Post it to your account immediately

Creators

Template builders who design the video formats buyers use.

  • Build templates using supported AI video models
  • Set your own price
  • Share your template link on TikTok or Reels
  • Earn revenue every time someone buys a generation

How distribution works

  • Creators post example results to their own TikTok or Reels
  • They put their Twinreel template link in their bio
  • Viewers tap the link, upload a photo, and buy their own version
  • Buyers post their result — creating more awareness for the template
  • The cycle repeats, driving more buyers back to the creator's link
